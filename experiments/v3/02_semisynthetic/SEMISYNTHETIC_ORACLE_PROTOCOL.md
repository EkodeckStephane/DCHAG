# DCHAG v3 real-anchored semi-synthetic intervention protocol

Experiment family: `V3-SS-*`

Protocol freeze date: 2026-08-21.

Status: **FROZEN before any semi-synthetic model-performance scoring**.

## 1. Scientific purpose

The LANL experiments establish external observational ingestibility, temporal trajectory construction, sparse P/T lagged recurrence, and predictive transportability. They cannot identify intervention effects because LANL supplies no defensive-intervention oracle.

This protocol introduces an explicit intervention oracle while retaining real operational temporal anchors. Its purpose is to test causal-effect recovery and defensive-control ranking without relabelling observational LANL behavior as causal truth.

The benchmark is **semi-synthetic**: exogenous temporal anchor sequences come from the immutable real LANL day-02 300-s trajectory, while H/P/T causal mechanisms, controls, nonlinear interactions, and the final compromise outcome are generated by a fully known stochastic structural causal model (SCM).

## 3. Unit allocation and leakage barrier

The retained trajectory contains `31,243` unique devices. Device allocation is deterministic and outcome-blind.

1. Sort all device identifiers by `SHA256("dchag-v3-ss-oracle-001-device-order|" + device)`.
2. Reserve the first `30,000` devices only.
3. Split them into **20 disjoint blocks of 1,500 devices**.
4. Blocks 0--3 are development worlds, one per workflow family.
5. Blocks 4--19 are confirmatory worlds, four independent blocks per workflow family.
6. The remaining `1,243` devices are unused and cannot be substituted post hoc.
7. Within each world, sort the 1,500 devices by `SHA256("dchag-v3-ss-oracle-001-split|" + world_id + "|" + device)`; first 1,100 are training units and final 400 are held-out test units.

No device can appear in more than one world.

## 4. Real-anchor extraction

Every device receives one six-step real anchor segment. The segment start is:

`SHA256("dchag-v3-ss-oracle-001-segment|" + world_id + "|" + device) mod 176`.

The six consecutive 300-s indices are `start..start+5`. Because the retained LANL file stores active device-windows only, a missing `(device, window_idx)` is reconstructed as an all-zero anchor row; no event is imputed.

Three binary exogenous anchor channels are retained:

- `A_person`: parent trajectory `H_present`;
- `A_process`: parent trajectory `P_present`;
- `A_technical`: parent trajectory `T_present`.

These variables are **operational anchors**, not causal H/P/T truth. In particular, `A_person` is not used as evidence that a person caused a downstream event.

## 5. Semi-synthetic SCM

All worlds use horizon `6` and the following 15 observed variables in temporal/event order:

`A_person, A_process, A_technical, R, C1, C2, C3, C4, H1, H2, P1, P2, T1, T2, Y`.

Types are:

- `A_*`: fixed observed exogenous real anchors;
- `R`: observed dynamic risk/context state;
- `C1..C4`: observed defensive controls;
- `H1,H2`: generated human-layer states;
- `P1,P2`: generated process-layer states;
- `T1,T2,Y`: generated technical-layer states, with `Y` the final compromise target.

The four workflow families are retained as semantic/topological families:

1. `helpdesk_identity`;
2. `bec_payment`;
3. `exfiltration`;
4. `itot_change`.

The family changes the admissible cross-branch edges and semantic aliases, but not the evaluation rules.

### 5.1 Dynamic context and treatment assignment

`R_t` is persistent, is influenced by the three real anchors, and may depend on prior defensive action. Each natural-policy control `Ck_t` is generated from observed current/past risk information, its own lag, real anchors, and selected prior technical/process states. Thus treatment assignment is non-random and time varying.

There is **no hidden common cause in this benchmark**. All variables that confound control assignment and generated outcomes are present in the public trajectory. Hidden-confounding stress tests belong to workstream `04_hidden_confounding` and must not be mixed into the RQ1 benchmark.

### 5.2 H/P/T propagation

Generated H, P, and T states use Bernoulli structural equations with family-specific parent masks, persistent lag-1 terms, real-anchor inputs, cross-layer branches, and protective control coefficients. At least one nonlinear pairwise interaction is present in each generated layer and at least two interacting branches reach `Y`.

World-specific coefficients and intercepts are sampled from frozen ranges using only the world seed. Confirmatory coefficients must never be tuned after inspection.

### 5.3 Link functions

Local Bernoulli mechanisms use a frozen family/node assignment among logistic, probit, complementary-log-log, and soft-threshold links. Probabilities are clipped to `[0.005, 0.995]`.

## 7. Intervention estimand and oracle

For each world and each `Ck`, the primary causal estimand is the average final-time risk reduction under a sustained binary intervention:

`ATE_k = E[Y_5(do(Ck_0:5 = 0)) - Y_5(do(Ck_0:5 = 1))]`.

Other controls remain under their natural structural assignment policy and are allowed to respond to intervened history. The three LANL anchor sequences remain fixed to the same real sequences under both regimes.

Oracle risks are computed with paired common random numbers over the same anchor units. The confirmatory oracle uses at least 100 Monte-Carlo replicates per anchor unit/regime unless an exact conditional expectation implementation is used. Oracle Monte-Carlo standard errors are retained.

The target estimand is therefore a well-defined SCM intervention contrast. It is **not** derived from LANL attack labels.

## 8. Development/confirmatory firewall

### Stage A — `V3-SS-DEV-001`

Only the four development worlds may be generated and scored while finalizing the semi-synthetic estimator implementation. Candidate settings must be enumerated before their development results are compared. Any selected estimator configuration and selection rule must be committed before Stage B begins.

### Stage B — `V3-SS-CONF-001`

The 16 confirmatory worlds are generated only after the estimator configuration is frozen. For each world:

1. generate public observed trajectories without exposing private SCM parameters to the estimator;
2. fit all frozen estimators using public training data only;
3. write effect estimates, trajectory predictions, selected edges, metadata, and hashes;
4. freeze those outputs;
5. only then compute/read oracle effects and true generated edges for scoring.

A confirmatory failure, negative result, or comparator win remains in the ledger.

## 9. Frozen comparators for RQ1

The confirmatory comparison must include at least:

- DCHAG learned sparse sequential estimator;
- dense sequential g-formula using all temporally admissible observed history;
- observational-association comparator.

The dense sequential g-formula is the strong causal comparator. The experiment is not designed to force DCHAG to beat it.

The final exact DCHAG screening-scale rule for this semi-synthetic sample size is selected in Stage A only and frozen before Stage B. The v2 hyperparameters are the starting reference, not a guarantee of transfer.

## 10. Confirmatory primary endpoints

Across the 16 independent confirmatory worlds, report:

1. world-level intervention-effect MAE for each estimator;
2. paired world-level DCHAG minus dense-g MAE difference with 95% world-block bootstrap CI and exact two-sided sign-flip p-value;
3. signed effect bias;
4. Kendall and Spearman control-ranking agreement;
5. normalized top-control regret and top-control accuracy;
6. DCHAG learned-edge precision/recall/F1, scored only after prediction/effect freeze;
7. prospective Brier score/Brier skill where defined.

The **16 worlds**, not the 64 world-control rows, are the independent units for DCHAG-vs-dense inference.

## 11. Secondary anchor diagnostics

Report per world and split:

- anchor prevalence for `A_person`, `A_process`, `A_technical`;
- fraction of six-step units containing all three anchor types at least once;
- number of all-zero anchor units;
- generated H/P/T/Y prevalences;
- natural-policy control prevalences;
- positivity diagnostics for every control by observed risk strata.

If positivity is materially violated, the finding is retained and the causal-recovery claim is narrowed rather than repaired post hoc.

## 13. Claim boundary

A successful confirmatory experiment may support **causal-effect recovery on an explicitly identified, real-trajectory-anchored semi-synthetic benchmark**. It cannot by itself establish causal effects in LANL, attacker intent in LANL, or real-world defensive-control effectiveness.

The observational LANL transportability evidence and the semi-synthetic causal-oracle evidence must remain separate in the manuscript and be combined only at the level of triangulated evidence.
