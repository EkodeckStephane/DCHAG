## Global manuscript boundary

The strongest defensible empirical position is: DCHAG provides an auditable typed temporal intervention model with competitive principal causal-effect fidelity, stable top-control choice in the tested semi-synthetic benchmark, measurable cross-family and hidden-confounding limits, and a controlled fit-time advantage over the tested scaling range. Real LANL and OpTC data support operational evidence mapping, temporal construction, and observational portability; the causal-effect claims remain restricted to the explicit semi-synthetic SCM benchmark.

## Mandatory negative/null disclosures

1. Principal RQ1 does not establish DCHAG superiority over dense-g.
2. Dense-g is slightly better on the principal held-out predictive Brier/BSS endpoint.
3. Cross-family transfer degrades both estimators and gives no DCHAG advantage.
4. Strong hidden confounding measurably degrades DCHAG causal-effect fidelity.
5. RQ4 top-control stability is shared by dense-g; it is not a DCHAG-specific advantage.
6. DCHAG has steeper observed scaling slopes and no consistent memory advantage.
7. The corrected LANL H endpoint remains weak/negative relative to the P/T evidence.
8. OpTC C1 failed completely due to timestamp normalization and remains retained in provenance.
